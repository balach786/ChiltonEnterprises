## Verified-correct (inspected, no change needed)

* **Indexes** — 150+ present, including every uniqueness rule (`uniq_movement_per_source`,
  `uniq_payment_income`, `uniq_advance_expense`, `uniq_active_token`, `uniq_confirmed_month`,
  `uniq_closing_per_day`, `uniq_default_account`, receipt-per-payment).
* **Transactions** — `transactionMode()` reports `transaction`; `integration/transactions.test.ts`
  asserts atomicity and rollback on the replica set.
* **Money** — integer paisa end to end, `splitEvenly` for the 28-month schedule, `serializeMoney`
  mirrors; no float arithmetic on money in either app.
* **RBAC** — 5 roles / 38 permissions, per-request `effectivePermissions()`, matrix verified live
  (see `docs/business-rules.md`).
* **Compression, helmet, rate limits, morgan, JSON body limits** — present in `app.ts`.
* **Config validation** — refuses to boot without `MONGODB_URI`/`JWT_SECRET` (≥16 chars) and never
  echoes secret values.
* **CNIC masking** — list/statement/export paths mask unless the caller holds `cnic:view`.
* **Audit trail** — actor, role, action, entity, previous/new value, reason, IP, user agent.
